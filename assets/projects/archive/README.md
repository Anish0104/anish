# Archived cover sources

Superseded material, kept rather than deleted. Nothing here is read by
`scripts/build-project-covers.mjs`.

## `screenshots/`

Captures of these projects actually running, taken when the covers were
screenshots. They are kept because they are real and hard to retake: two of
them need credentials or a service that is no longer reachable.

Nothing here is built into the site. The covers are drawings now, authored in
`scripts/project-cover-art.mjs`, matched to the VTrack traffic frame that
remains the one photographic cover.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `semantic-search.png` | The FastAPI service over the committed 10k MS MARCO index, showing a real query and the reranked passages with their scores. |
| `docpilot.png` | The Streamlit app against its committed ChromaDB store. |
| `vouch.png` | The live deployment at `vouch-q017.onrender.com`. |
| `quantvision.png` | A saved strategy figure from a cell of the project's own capstone notebook. |

## `svg-covers/`

The abstract node-diagram covers these replaced, in their light and dark
variants. Kept only as a record of what the cards used to look like.

## Still current

`../vtrack-source.png` is the one source the build still reads: the annotated
traffic frame that is the visual reference for the whole set.
